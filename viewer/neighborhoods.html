Neighborhood prints

Every Philadelphia neighborhood, cut from Pixel Philly's map. Download one to print for your wall or use as a desktop or phone wallpaper.

Free for personal use. Print one for your home, or use it as your desktop or phone wallpaper. Please don't sell the images or use them for commercial purposes. These are imperfect. They are visual representations of these neighborhoods circa 2022-24 based on public data, processed by Claude. These images should be used for entertainment purposes only.

Each comes in three sizes:

  • Print is the full-resolution cut-out, 1 map pixel per image pixel. At 300 dots per inch, 90 of the 159 print at least 18 inches on their long side; the smallest, Woodland Terrace, prints about 5.5 inches.
  • Desktop is the whole neighborhood at 5,120 by 2,880 pixels, which also suits 4K screens.
  • Phone is a crop from the middle of the neighborhood that fills a 1,290 by 2,796 screen.

      The map is an AI-generated illustration made from public data. It's imperfect and incomplete, so don't use it for navigation or measurements. Neighborhood boundaries are informal, compiled from public maps and user feedback; they come from OpenDataPhilly's Philadelphia neighborhoods, by Robert Cheetham, licensed CC BY 4.0. Buildings and trees along a boundary go to the neighborhood where they stand. Areas are measured from the boundaries. More on the map's sources is on the about page.
